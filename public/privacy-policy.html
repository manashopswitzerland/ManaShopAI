Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mana AI Hub, operated by Mana Shop and Mana Kendra (Switzerland), collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our AI assistant through Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, or our website chat.

1. Information We Collect

When you message our AI assistant, we may collect:

  • Your messages and conversation history
  • Your platform user ID (e.g. Facebook sender ID)
  • Contact information you voluntarily provide (phone number, email address)
  • The store or channel you contacted us through

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • To improve our AI assistant's responses
  • To follow up on service or product inquiries you initiate
  • To connect you with a human support agent when requested

3. Data Storage

Conversation data is stored securely in our database. We retain conversations for support and quality purposes. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the assistant:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — for Messenger and Instagram DM delivery
  • OpenAI — for AI-generated responses
  • Twilio — for WhatsApp messaging
  • MongoDB — for data storage

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data handling.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at info@mana-shop.ch. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

Our web chat widget may use browser session storage to maintain conversation continuity. We do not use tracking cookies.
